# Positioning & Messaging

This doc records **what the site says today**: whom it addresses, what it promises, how it backs that up, and where each call to action leads. It was recovered from page JSON under `src/data/`. Nothing here is approved strategy unless it is marked **approved**. Product purpose, the approved ICP and the conversion goal are in [VISION.md](VISION.md); this doc doesn't repeat them. Competitors and substitutes are in [COMPETITORS.md](COMPETITORS.md). Voice and terminology are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md#brand-voice--terminology). Search intent is in [SEO.md](SEO.md).

**Status labels:** **approved** = confirmed by a human (source and date given) · **observed** = what the live copy says, with file evidence · **inferred** = a reading of the copy, not confirmed · **proposed** = a hypothesis, not approved.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Product positioning (this doc) and campaign targeting are separate. A campaign page such as `src/data/global/textlocal-alternative.json` (Textlocal switchers) or `src/data/global/hello-for-startups.json` (startups under $50k revenue) targets one audience. Neither redefines the product. Put campaign-specific audiences in the campaign's own page JSON and a row in [Campaigns](#campaigns-separate-from-product-positioning), not in the audience list.

## Category & Alternatives

- **Category as the site states it (observed):** "cloud communication platform" (home title, `src/data/global/home.json` `HeadComp.title`: "Best Cloud Communication Platform for Businesses | MSG91") and "customer solution platform" (home banner tagline). Applications use other categories: Hello is "Customer Support Software" / "AI-Powered Contact Center", Segmento is "Customer Data Platform", and Campaign is "Event based automation" (`home.json` `ProductsComp`).
- **Inferred category:** a CPaaS (messaging APIs) with customer-engagement applications on top. The home page mixes both framings, and no single category line is approved.
- **Alternatives:** named direct competitors (comparison pages exist for Twilio, MessageBird, Interakt, WATI, AiSensy, Freshchat, Intercom, Tawk, DelightChat). Also substitutes such as staying with the current vendor, building in-house, or using spreadsheets and manual WhatsApp. Facts, sources and dates are kept only in [COMPETITORS.md](COMPETITORS.md).

## Proposed (not approved)

These are hypotheses for the marketing owner. Each would need approval and, where it affects conversion, an experiment ([EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md)).

- **P1:** Channel banners that name the buyer's situation ("Add OTP verification to your sign-up in minutes") may convert better than generic headings ("Seamless Global messaging"). Test on one channel page first.
- **P2:** Passing product context to sign-up (`/signup?service=<product>`) would shorten step 3 and allow sign-ups to be attributed to a product page. This needs the sign-up owner, because a sign-up rewrite is in progress on `web`.
- **P3:** RCS "Get Started" should either say "Book a demo" or go to sign-up, so the label matches the destination.
- **P4:** Pick one canonical figure for each scale claim (C1, C4, C8). Keep it in one data source and reference it, instead of repeating it in 127 files.
- **P5:** Retire or refresh the Textlocal page. If retiring, add a redirect to the most relevant page, not the home page ([SEO.md](SEO.md)).

## Unresolved Strategic Questions

2. Is MSG91 positioned as India-first or global? The home FAQ says "in India". Country pages are mostly copies of global.
3. Which products lead? The menu gives Hello, Campaign and Segmento as much prominence as the channels. No priority order is recorded ([VISION.md](VISION.md#strategic-priorities--success-measures)).
4. Who owns and verifies the scale numbers (C1–C8), and how often?
5. Should "free" CTAs link to defined free-plan terms?
6. Who approves new comparison pages and competitor claims?
