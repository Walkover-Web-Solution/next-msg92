## **Why Black Friday breaks unprepared customer service**

Before the solution, understand the problem. Black Friday overwhelms customer service in specific ways:

**Volume explodes suddenly.** You don't get a gentle ramp-up. Volume spikes hard and fast, often several times your normal load, concentrated into a short window. A team sized for normal days simply can't answer everyone in time.

**Customers expect instant replies.** Black Friday shoppers are in a hurry and spoiled for choice. A question that goes unanswered for even a short time often means the sale goes to whoever replied first.

**The questions are repetitive — but relentless.** "Is this in stock?", "Does the discount apply to this?", "When will it arrive if I order now?", "What payment methods do you accept?" The same questions, thousands of times over.

**It happens around the clock.** Black Friday deals drive activity late into the night and across the weekend into Cyber Monday — long after your team has gone home.

**Cart abandonment surges.** With so many options and time pressure, shoppers abandon carts more than ever — and without fast follow-up, those sales are lost.

The common thread: it's a volume-and-speed problem. And that's exactly what a chatbot is built to solve.

---

## **How a chatbot saves your Black Friday**

A chatbot addresses every one of those pressure points at once:

**It handles unlimited conversations simultaneously.** A chatbot answers 10 or 10,000 customers at the same speed. The spike that would overwhelm a human team is just a normal moment for automation — no queues, no waiting.

**It answers instantly, 24/7.** Every customer gets an immediate response, at any hour, through the entire Black Friday weekend — capturing sales your offline team would miss.

**It resolves the repetitive questions automatically.** Stock, discounts, shipping times, payment options — the bot handles the flood of routine questions on its own, so your human team is freed for the conversations that truly need them.

**It recovers abandoned carts.** The bot can automatically follow up with shoppers who left items behind, on WhatsApp where they'll see it, and guide them back to complete the purchase.

**It scales without temporary hires.** Instead of recruiting, training, and paying temporary staff for a few days, your automation absorbs the surge — at no extra per-conversation cost.

The result: you capture more of the demand Black Friday generates, with less stress and lower cost, instead of losing sales to slow replies.

## **Best practices to get it right**

**Don't over-automate.** Even at peak volume, always give customers an easy path to a human. Automation should feel like fast service, not a wall.

**Keep answers accurate and updated.** Make sure your bot has the current Black Friday prices, promotions, and stock status. An outdated answer during peak season costs sales.

**Prioritize speed to purchase.** Use in-chat capabilities so customers can move from question to purchase with as little friction as possible. Hello's chatbot supports in-chat ordering, keeping shoppers in the conversation.

**Have the human handoff ready.** When the bot reaches its limit, a smooth transfer to a person with full context keeps high-value conversations alive.

**Watch your data.** Peak season generates huge insight — what customers ask, where they drop off. Use it during and after to keep improving.
