## **Frequently Asked Questions** 

1\. What is MSG91's WhatsApp Business API and how is it different from the standard WhatsApp Business app?

The standard WhatsApp Business app runs on a single device, supports one active user, has limited automation, and cannot broadcast to more than 256 contacts. MSG91's WhatsApp Business API is scalable infrastructure that supports multiple agents through Hello, full chatbot automation, large-scale broadcast campaigns, deep CRM integration, and delivery analytics. 2\. Can I use my existing WhatsApp number with MSG91's API?

Yes, provided the number is not actively registered on the standard WhatsApp mobile or desktop app. Numbers used on the API cannot be simultaneously used on the standard app. If migrating from another Business Service Provider, WhatsApp allows BSP migration with access to your WhatsApp Business Manager and ownership verification.
